Services

Full-Stack Business Platform

A complete web platform built from scratch for your business. Public website, service or product listings, booking or enquiry system, admin panel, lead management, analytics and newsletter system. Everything you need to operate and grow in one place.

Admin Panel and CMS

A fully custom, password-protected admin panel that lets you manage your content, upload media, track leads, reply to enquiries, add private notes, export data and view analytics. No technical knowledge required. Built specifically for the way your business operates.

Lead Generation System

Enquiry forms, booking tabs, newsletter popups, push notifications and SMS alerts all working together. Every lead is captured, stored, scored and exportable. You own your audience completely, no reliance on social media.

Email Marketing System

Automated welcome emails, new listing alerts to your entire subscriber list, lead follow-up confirmation emails and newsletter blasts all built directly into your admin panel. Powered by Resend. Your audience hears from you without you lifting a finger.

Progressive Web App

Your platform converted into a fully installable PWA. Visitors can add it to their phone home screen like a native app — no App Store required. Faster load times, offline support and a premium feel that sets you apart from every competitor.

Website Rebuild

Already have a site that feels outdated or underperforming? I rebuild it from the ground up with a modern design, full backend and every feature your business needs, without losing what makes your brand yours.

Why Work With Me

Every day without the right website
is a client you will never know you lost.

Think about this.

A potential client finds you online at 11pm. They are interested. They want to know more about what you offer. They click your link and land on a slow, generic page with no clear services, no way to enquire without texting you directly, nothing that signals you are serious.

So they do not reach out. They scroll on. They find someone else.

That happens every single day to businesses without the right website. Not because they are bad at what they do, but because their online presence is not working while they sleep.
01 🌙

Leads while you sleep

Your services live on dedicated pages with clear descriptions, pricing context and a contact form. A client can discover your business at midnight and send you an enquiry without you lifting a finger. You wake up to a lead that is already warmed up.

02 📊

Know exactly what is working

Every time someone visits a page or submits an enquiry, it is tracked. You can see exactly which services or offers are getting the most attention. No more guessing what is working.

03 📧

You own your audience

When a visitor lands on your site, a subtle popup invites them to join your list. Now you have their email. When you launch a new offer or want to stay top of mind, you have a ready audience to reach. You own that relationship. Social media does not.

04 🏆

Every win becomes social proof

When you complete a project or close a deal, you update it in your admin panel. It shows up on your site automatically. That is not just a content update. That is proof that you deliver results.

05 📱

Content that looks good when shared

Your pages look stunning when shared on WhatsApp, Instagram or LinkedIn, with a proper image, title and description pulled automatically. Not a broken link. Not a blank preview.

06 🔍

Show up when they search for you

When someone asks a friend "do you know a good [your profession]?" and your name comes up, they Google you. They find a website that looks like you mean business. And they reach out.

The businesses winning online right now are not necessarily the best at what they do.

They are the ones who show up professionally everywhere a client looks. They are the ones capturing leads while their competitors are waiting for referrals. They are the ones whose content gets shared because it looks good enough to share.

You already do the hard part: delivering your service, building your reputation, showing up every day.

I just make sure the internet is doing its job for you while you do yours.
